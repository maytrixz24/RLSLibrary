# RLSlibrary — Initial 10-book catalogue and rights notes

## Catalogue target

The initial catalogue contains 10 entries total: the two owner-supplied books plus eight public-domain or openly reusable additions. Every one of the six requested categories is represented.

The eight additions are linked to source-hosted PDFs or openly reusable source editions. RLSlibrary links to those source files rather than repackaging copyrighted modern editions. The exact PDF and source links are recorded in \`data/books.json\`.

## Reader-rating methodology

The eight additions were selected using Goodreads reader ratings in the 4.0–5.0 range, checked on 27 November 2026. Ratings are user-generated community averages and are not academic peer review, editorial endorsement, or a guarantee that every claim in a historical work is correct. Counts and ratings change over time, and specific editions/translations can receive different scores.

## Distribution and rights standard

The intended jurisdiction for the initial owner-managed library is Indonesia. The current Indonesian Copyright Act is Law No. 28 of 2014.

- Article 58(1) generally protects books and other written works for the author's life plus 70 years, calculated from 1 January of the year after death.
- Article 59(1)(g) gives translations, interpretations, alterations, anthologies, databases, adaptations, arrangements and modifications a separate 50-year term from first publication.

The original text, translator, first-publication date and any new editorial material must be checked separately. A modern cover, introduction, notes, annotations, translation or typesetting may carry separate rights. The catalogue uses historical public-domain editions or openly licensed material and links to the source. Do not substitute a modern commercial edition.

The website is public on the internet. Public-domain status under Indonesian law does not automatically establish public-domain status in every country. The catalogue records source/edition notes and links back to the source. Readers outside Indonesia should check the law applying to them.

## Edition-specific notes

- **The Elements of Style:** use the Geneseo open-textbook PDF and retain the reuse notices included in that edition.
- **Meditations:** the linked scan is the 1882 George Long edition. The Library of Congress catalogue identifies the edition as public domain and free to use and reuse.
- **The Federalist Papers:** the eCampusOntario edition identifies the underlying essays as public domain. Its supplementary front and back matter is licensed CC BY 4.0, so retain its attribution notice. It is a historical U.S. constitutional text, not current New Zealand law.
- **Pride and Prejudice:** the linked scan is an 1894 historical edition of Jane Austen's novel.
- **Jane Eyre:** the Toronto Metropolitan University edition uses a c. 1900 W. Nicholson & Sons edition. The university states that the novel is free of known copyright restrictions; supplementary front and back matter is CC BY 4.0.
- **On the Origin of Species:** the Smithsonian catalogue identifies the source as Darwin's 1859 first edition and states CC0 / no copyright restrictions. The linked PDF is an archival scan.
- **Relativity:** the PDF reproduces the 1920 English translation by Robert W. Lawson. The provider warns that U.S. public-domain status does not necessarily settle rights in every country. For Indonesia, translations generally receive a 50-year term from first publication under Article 59(1)(g) of Law No. 28 of 2014; this 1920 edition appears to be outside that term. Check local rules before redistribution elsewhere.
- **The Dhammapada:** the 1881 F. Max Müller scan is part of Sacred Books of the East, Vol. X and includes the Dhammapada followed by the Sutta-Nipata. The linked file therefore contains both parts of the historical volume.

## Important context labels

- *The Federalist Papers* is a historical American constitutional/political text, not New Zealand legal advice or a statement of current New Zealand law.
- *The Dhammapada* is one historical English translation of a Buddhist text; other traditions and translations differ.
- *On the Origin of Species* is a foundational historical scientific work. Modern biology has developed substantially since its publication.
- *Relativity* is a historical popular exposition; scientific understanding should be supplemented with current materials.
- *Meditations* is a philosophical work, not a modern psychology or clinical guide.

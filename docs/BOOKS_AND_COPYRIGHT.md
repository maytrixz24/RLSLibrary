# RLSlibrary — Initial 10-book catalogue and rights notes

## Catalogue target

The initial catalogue contains 10 entries total: the two owner-supplied books plus eight public-domain/openly reusable additions. Every one of the six requested categories is represented.

## Reader-rating methodology

The eight additions were selected using Goodreads reader ratings in the 4.0–5.0 range, checked on 27 November 2026. Ratings are user-generated community averages and are not academic peer review, editorial endorsement, or a guarantee that every claim in a historical work is correct. Counts and ratings change over time, and specific editions/translations can receive different scores.

## Distribution and rights standard

The intended jurisdiction for the initial owner-managed library is Indonesia. The current Indonesian Copyright Act is Law No. 28 of 2014.

- Article 58(1) generally protects books and other written works for the author's life plus 70 years, calculated from 1 January of the year after death.
- Article 59(1)(g) gives translations, interpretations, alterations, anthologies, databases, adaptations, arrangements and modifications a separate 50-year term from first publication.

The original text, translator, first-publication date and any new editorial material must be checked separately. A modern cover, introduction, notes, annotations, translation or typesetting may carry separate rights. The catalogue should use only the cited historical public-domain text/edition or an explicitly open-licensed PDF; do not substitute a modern commercial edition.

The website is public on the internet. Public-domain status under Indonesian law does not automatically establish public-domain status in every country. The catalogue records source/edition notes and links back to the source. Readers outside Indonesia should check the law applying to them.

## Important context labels

- *The Federalist Papers* is a historical American constitutional/political text, not New Zealand legal advice or a statement of current New Zealand law.
- *The Dhammapada* is one historical English translation of a Buddhist text; other traditions and translations differ.
- *On the Origin of Species* is a foundational historical scientific work. Modern biology has developed substantially since its publication.
- *Relativity* is a historical popular exposition; scientific understanding should be supplemented with current materials.
- *Meditations* is a philosophical work, not a modern psychology or clinical guide.
